Portfolio checklist

What goes in the repository.

Every project

  • Problem and intended user in one paragraph.
  • Architecture diagram and key trade-offs.
  • Evaluation: baseline, metric, result, failure slices.
  • One honest limitation and one next step.
  • Reproducible setup and a demo.

Lead with three

  • Docs Answer Engine — RAG, citations, evals.
  • Research Copilot — tools, approvals, traces.
  • AI Product Capstone — deployed, monitored, case study.

What to avoid

  • Notebooks with no README or evaluation.
  • Tutorial copies with a new colour palette.
  • Claims without a measurement.
  • “It works” with no failure analysis.
