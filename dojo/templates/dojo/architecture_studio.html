Why this matters now

The work AI makes more valuable.

When implementation accelerates, the bottleneck moves to decisions, integration, verification, and trust.

01

Problem framing

Translate a vague business pain into constraints, non-goals, and testable outcomes. Generated code cannot save a badly framed problem.

02

Domain modeling

Discover the nouns, invariants, and state transitions that the business actually runs on. This is where most hidden complexity lives.

03

Boundaries

Decide what belongs together, what must stay independent, and what should be a service, module, event, or simple function.

04

Data and contracts

Own the source of truth, schema evolution, API compatibility, and the semantics of failure. Data outlives code.

05

Reliability and security

Design for retries, partial failure, abuse, least privilege, and incident response. These are not post-launch chores.

06

Verification and communication

Prove behaviour with tests, evals, fitness functions, and observability — then explain the trade-off in language others can challenge.

What AI changes

The cost of producing a first draft.

  • Boilerplate, CRUD, tests, and migrations appear faster.
  • Exploration gets cheaper; more alternatives can be sketched.
  • Knowledge becomes easier to retrieve at the point of need.
  • Review, integration, and verification become the constraint.

What it does not change

Accountability for the system.

  • Who decides what data is true and who may see it.
  • Which trade-offs are acceptable for the business.
  • How the system fails, recovers, and explains itself.
  • Who owns the consequences when a generated change is wrong.

How to use a lab

Do the thinking before reading the answer.

01Read the brief

Requirements, scale, constraints. Note what is deliberately missing.

02Sketch the shape

Components, data ownership, sync/async boundaries, failure modes.

03Compare decisions

Where does the reference disagree with you? Why?

04Write an ADR

Record one decision, its alternatives, and what would make you revisit it.
